Workshops

Free, hands-on, and genuinely messy.

The curriculum

What students actually learn

A full workshop runs about two hours and moves through four stages. Shorter classroom visits cover the first two.

  1. STAGE 01

    Hardware disassembly

    Goggles and lab coats on. Students open a donated desktop or laptop and pull it down to the board — drive, RAM, CPU, fan, power supply — learning what each component does and how to handle it without damaging it.

  2. STAGE 02

    Troubleshooting & repair

    Now put it back. We teach the diagnostic habit: change one thing, test, repeat. Students learn why a machine won't post, how to swap a drive or stick of RAM, and how to tell "broken" from "just needs cleaning".

  3. STAGE 03

    Cybersecurity basics

    Once you've held the drive in your hand, data stops being abstract. We cover strong passwords, phishing, what "the cloud" physically is, safe habits online, and how to properly erase a device before you give it away.

  4. STAGE 04

    Upcycle build

    The parts that can't be reused become material. Students design and build something to take home — a disc clock, a board sculpture, a set of platter coasters. It's the part they talk about on the way out.

Host a workshop

Bring eWasteGang to your school or community centre

We travel to schools, libraries, after-school programmes, churches, scout troops and community centres across Montgomery County and the surrounding area. There's no fee. Here's exactly how it goes.

  1. 1

    Send us the basics

    Use the form here or email us. We need your organisation, roughly how many students, their ages, and two or three dates that could work.

  2. 2

    We'll call to scope it

    A fifteen-minute conversation. We'll confirm which session fits your group and how long we have — anything from a 45-minute classroom visit to a full two-hour lab.

  3. 3

    You provide the room

    Tables that can get messy, chairs, power outlets, and one staff member or chaperone in the room. A gym, cafeteria, library or classroom all work fine.

  4. 4

    We bring everything else

    Donated machines, tools, gloves, safety goggles, lab coats, table covering, and mentors. We set up before your students arrive and clean up after.

  5. 5

    Workshop day

    Students build something, take it home, and we haul away any e-waste your building wants gone. If you'd like photos for your newsletter, tell us in advance and we'll respect whatever media policy you have.
